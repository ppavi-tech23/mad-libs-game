# mad-libs-game 🎭

A fun and simple Python Mad Libs game where you enter nouns, adjectives, and verbs to generate a silly story! Perfect for beginners learning input handling and string formatting in Python.

---

## 🎯 How to Play

1. Run the script: `python mad_libs.py`
2. Enter the requested words when prompted (like a noun, adjective, verb, etc.).
3. Watch as your inputs are turned into a hilarious, custom story!

Example:
Enter a noun: cat
Enter an adjective: fluffy
Enter a verb: dance
Enter a noun: moon
Enter a verb: sing

Output:
> Once upon a time, there was a fluffy cat who loved to dance all day...
>
> One day, moon walked into the room and caught the cat in the act.
>
> moon: "What are you doing?"
> cat: "I'm just dancing, what's the big deal?"
> moon: "Well, it's not every day that you see a cat dancing in the middle of the day."
> cat: "I guess you're right. Maybe I should take a break."
> moon: "That's probably a good idea. Why don't we go sing instead?"
> cat: "Sure, that sounds like fun!"
>
> And so, moon and the cat went off to sing and had a great time.
> The end.
